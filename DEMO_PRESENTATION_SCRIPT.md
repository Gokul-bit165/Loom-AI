# 🛡️ Executive Q&A & Objection Handling Playbook

### Q1 (From Managing Director / Owner): "How do I know this won't hallucinate numbers like ChatGPT?"
**Your Answer**: 
> "That is the core architectural principle of Loom AI: **The Zero LLM Calculation Rule**.
> No number, no rupee total, no OEE percentage, and no shortfall metre is calculated by an AI language model. All mathematics are executed deterministically using PostgreSQL and SQLAlchemy according to the mill's standard formulas:
> $$\text{Target Metres} = \frac{\text{RPM} \times \text{Minutes}}{\text{PPI} \times 39.37}$$
> The AI engine is strictly used as an analytical interpreter to turn verified mathematical tables into plain English executive verdicts. It cannot hallucinate numbers because it does not compute them."

### Q2 (From Weaving Superintendent): "Will my supervisors spend hours feeding data into this?"
**Your Answer**:
> "Quite the opposite. Currently, supervisors spend 45 to 60 minutes every single shift filling out manual paper logs and Excel sheets. With Loom AI, data ingestion takes less than 2 minutes via automated shift dump or our streamlined batch entry. Loom AI frees up 550 hours of supervisor time annually so they can stay on the shop floor fixing looms instead of sitting in front of Excel."

### Q3 (From Maintenance Engineer): "How does the Root Cause Analysis know why a loom stopped?"
**Your Answer**:
> "Loom AI combines three data layers: the machine's electronic stop error codes, high-frequency telemetry (motor amperage, vibration, air pressure), and contextual operational data (yarn lot number, style standard, ambient humidity). It structures findings into Observed sensor facts, Inferred mechanical correlations, and Predicted failure risk. It doesn't guess; it correlates."

### Q4 (From CFO): "What is the total cost of ownership and IT requirements?"
**Your Answer**:
> "Loom AI is completely lightweight. It can run 100% on-premises on your local factory network, or in a private cloud container. There is zero hardware replacement required on your existing Toyota, Tsudakoma, or Picanol looms. The pilot runs on a standard desktop server, requiring no expensive IT infrastructure upgrades."
